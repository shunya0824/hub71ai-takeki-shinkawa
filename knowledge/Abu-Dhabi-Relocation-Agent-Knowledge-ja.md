## AD-002 Sequence of Relocation Procedures

The starting point for relocation is a residency route, such as local employment, business ownership or freelancing, family sponsorship, or investment. The selected route determines who submits the application, which documents are required, and who pays the costs.

### Basic Sequence and the Reasons Behind It

For an adult relocating to the UAE and setting up a resident mobile line, bank account, and a residential tenancy paid by cheque, the basic sequence is to apply for the visa before arrival, enter the UAE once the eVisa is issued, and then proceed as follows: **obtain a visitor mobile number → attend the residency medical screening with the eVisa and other documents → register fingerprints → complete residency procedures and receive Emirates ID → convert the mobile line to resident registration → open a bank account → order a cheque book → select a permanent home and sign the tenancy contract**.

| Step | Procedure | What to Prepare and Why It Comes Next |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Obtain a mobile number with a visitor SIM or eSIM | A passport can be used for identity verification before obtaining Emirates ID. Present a valid visa or entry information when required. Establish a way to receive calls and SMS messages first |
| 2 | Attend residency medical screening | Bring the original passport and the eVisa or entry permit for the employment or residency application. Save electronic documents as PDFs and prepare copies for submission |
| 3 | Register fingerprints and biometrics for Emirates ID | Adults required to provide biometrics for their first application should book the appointment according to the application instructions and attend at the specified time |
| 4 | Receive the residence permit and Emirates ID | Complete the required medical screening, biometrics, insurance, and other steps, then check ICP for the application status and ID issuance and collection instructions |
| 5 | Convert the mobile line to resident registration | Use a valid Emirates ID to update the identity verification from visitor to resident status. Supported plans allow the same number to be retained |
| 6 | Open a resident bank account | Provide the bank's required documents, including Emirates ID, residency and passport documents, and income information. The mobile number is also used for SMS verification and communication from the bank |
| 7 | Order a cheque book | Choose an account with a cheque book that can be used to pay rent, then request issuance and delivery after opening the account. Establish the expected delivery date before agreeing on rental payment dates |
| 8 | Select a permanent home and sign the tenancy contract | Agree on the rent, number and dates of payments, deposit, and registration method. Prepare the required cheques and upfront payments to proceed with the contract. Utility payments and other arrangements follow |

### Why Emirates ID and Cheques Are Important Milestones in Establishing Daily Life

- **Mobile service:** A visitor number can be obtained without Emirates ID. Once the ID is issued, update identity verification and customer status to resident with the telecom provider. To keep the same number, switch to a plan that supports retaining it.
- **Banking:** Emirates ID is required to open a standard resident current account. After receiving the ID, submit the bank's required documents and open an account with a cheque book for rental payments.
- **Cheques:** Residential tenancies paid in instalments require post-dated cheques bearing the agreed payment dates when the contract is signed. Open the account and arrange the cheque book in advance so they are ready for the contract.
- **Tenancy:** Before signing, agree with the landlord on the payment method, number of payments, and due dates. For cheque payments, prepare the required number of cheques. If delivery will be too late, negotiate an alternative such as a bank transfer and record it in the contract. Property searches, enquiries, and viewings can proceed alongside ID and cheque preparations. The contract is signed after obtaining the ID.

For residency medical screening, prepare the eVisa or entry permit corresponding to the employment or residency procedure. Obtain the documents from the person handling the application and bring them with the original passport. Stay in temporary accommodation while arranging the medical screening and ID, then move into the permanent home once the bank account, cheques, and tenancy contract are ready.

### Preparations That Often Take Time

- Obtaining, attesting, and translating marriage, birth, and educational documents issued abroad.
- Securing school places and determining the child's year or grade placement.
- Bank identity checks, application review, and cheque book preparation.
- Coordinating the move-in date with utility connections and movers.
- Pet import documents and airline transport bookings.

Another approach is for the main applicant to travel first, arrange residency, housing, and schools, and have the family join later. This can reduce temporary accommodation costs for the family, while involving a period of living apart.

## AD-003 Residency Through Employment

The employer's HR or visa officer is the starting point for an employee's residency procedures. Sign the employment contract and coordinate the entry date, medical screening, residence application, and ID issuance schedule with the person responsible.

### Main Relocation-Related Employment Benefits

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Residency procedures | Employment-based entry and residency, medical screening, and ID |
| Insurance | Employee enrolment, availability of family coverage, and coverage start date |
| Housing | Housing allowance, company-provided accommodation, and a hotel immediately after arrival |
| Travel | Flights for the employee and family, and how often they are provided |
| Moving | Shipment of belongings, cost limits, and reimbursement |
| Education | School fee support, number of children covered, and annual limits |

The upfront funds an employee needs differ between a housing allowance included in salary and accommodation contracted directly by the company. When an annual allowance is paid monthly, a separate lump sum is needed for the first rental payment.

## AD-004 Family Residence Permits

The general income threshold for family sponsorship is AED4,000 per month, or AED3,000 when the employer provides accommodation. Requirements also concern the sponsor's residency, family relationship, housing, and insurance.

### Main Documents

- Sponsor's passport, residence permit, and Emirates ID.
- Employment and salary evidence.
- Family members' passports and identification photographs.
- Marriage certificate for the spouse.
- Birth certificates for children.
- Required attestations and translations.
- Required proof of accommodation.
- Health insurance for the family members concerned.

The general family sponsorship scheme includes a spouse, unmarried daughters, and sons under the age of 25.

### Sequence

Establish the main applicant's residency and sponsorship eligibility → prepare family relationship documents → arrange housing and insurance → complete family entry and residency procedures → complete medical screening and ID procedures for those required to do so.

School enquiries and visits can begin before the family's residency procedures are completed. Popular schools fill quickly, so start early.

## AD-005 Golden Residency for Property Investors

Golden Residency for property investors lasts **10 years**. ICP guidance sets the property investment threshold at a combined value of **AED2,000,000 or more**.

- A route is available using a mortgage from an approved local bank.
- A route is available using off-plan property from an approved developer.
- Prepare proof of property ownership or purchase and the documents required for the relevant route.
- Valid health insurance for the applicant and family is also relevant.
- Obtain documents showing the purchase or ownership of the qualifying property and apply for Golden Residency.

For the application, organise the ownership details, registration and purchase documents, whether the property is completed or off-plan, and the mortgage terms.

## AD-008 Document Attestation and Translation

Documents issued abroad follow the required attestation route through the competent authorities in the issuing country and UAE diplomatic missions. MOFA provides the UAE-side attestation service.

- MOFA attestation for personal documents costs **AED150 per document**.
- Degree certificates and transcripts are treated as separate documents.
- Attestation in the foreign country, translation, and delivery are separate costs.
- Documents in languages other than English or Arabic require an official translation meeting the receiving authority's requirements.
- Do not laminate originals intended for attestation.

Marriage and birth certificates are particularly important for family relocation. The issuing country, attestations already completed, and availability of translations determine the remaining procedures.

### Attestation at the UAE Embassy in the Home Country or Document-Issuing Country

**Marriage certificates, birth certificates, educational certificates, and graduation certificates used for visa and family applications may require attestation by the UAE embassy or consulate in the home country.** More precisely, the relevant country is the document's issuing country rather than the applicant's nationality. For example, a degree from a university in another country follows that country's attestation route.

The general sequence is: **required notarisation or certification by the competent authorities in the issuing country → attestation by the issuing country's foreign ministry or equivalent → attestation by the UAE embassy or consulate in that country → UAE MOFA attestation**. The preliminary steps depend on the country and document type, and digital or integrated procedures are available in some cases.

The procedure commonly described as "notarisation at the embassy" is formally called **consular attestation**, which verifies signatures and seals on documents. Complete the required translation and issuing-country attestations, then apply for attestation at the UAE embassy or consulate.

Prepare the marriage certificate for a spouse's family application. Prepare educational certificates when they appear on the document list for the occupation, qualification, or visa being applied for. Completing the issuing-country and UAE embassy attestations before departure reduces the need to send originals back after relocation.

## AD-009 Residency Medical Screening and Emirates ID

Book the medical examination for a residence application as medical fitness or visa screening. Use SEHA Visa Screening / Visafit to select a facility and appointment suitable for the residence application.

### Main Documents and Steps

- Original passport.
- Entry and residence application information.
- Identity documents such as Emirates ID, where applicable.
- Registration, photograph, consent, medical history, physical examination, and specified tests.
- Blood tests, chest X-rays, and other examinations are performed according to the requirements for the applicant and procedure.

Book Emirates ID identity verification and biometric registration according to the application instructions. After medical screening, confirm that the result has been issued, proceed with the residence application, and check ICP for ID issuance and collection instructions.

## AD-010 UAE PASS and Mobile Numbers

UAE PASS is used for digital identity verification, login, and electronic signatures across government and private-sector services. Registration options include Emirates ID, GCC ID, and passport. The required level of identity verification depends on the service.

### Mobile Contracts

Immediately after arrival, a tourist SIM or eSIM registered using a passport is an option. After obtaining residency and Emirates ID, resident plans become available. The visitor-plan number can be retained. du and Etisalat are major providers.

- Use a long-term number consistently for SMS messages from banks and government services.
- Tourist SIMs need to be renewed within 28 days.
- If the number changes, update the number registered with banks, UAE PASS, government services, and the tenancy contract.
- Complete account registration and identity verification in the UAE PASS app.

### Food and Household Essentials Delivery

Use Careem for food delivery and home delivery of household essentials.

### Comparing Temporary Accommodation

Important factors include travel to work, the areas scheduled for viewings, a kitchen, laundry facilities, parking, the price of extending the stay, and whether cleaning and utilities are included. A low room rate can be offset by daily long-distance travel and eating out.

For proof of accommodation in a family application, prepare the tenancy registration documents or other documents specified by the receiving authority. Request issuance from the property management company when signing the tenancy contract.

Property Finder and Bayut are useful apps that bring together property sale and rental listings. Listings can be filtered by contract type, such as yearly or monthly.

## AD-013 Rent, Payment Frequency, and Upfront Costs

Abu Dhabi rentals are often compared on an annual-rent basis. The contract determines payment arrangements, such as a single payment or multiple post-dated cheques.

### Main Expenses

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| First rental payment | The amount depends on the annual rent and number of payments |
| Deposit | 5-10% of the rent |
| Agency fee | Payment to the agency: 5.25% of the rent, including VAT |
| Tenancy registration | The responsible payer depends on the location, system, and contract |
| Utility deposits and connections | Based on electricity, water, cooling, and other service contracts |
| Furniture and appliances | Depends on whether the property is furnished and what is provided |
| Moving and temporary accommodation | Depends on the coordination of delivery access and the move-in date |

### Calculations

- Each equal rental instalment = annual rent / number of payments.
- Monthly equivalent rent = annual rent / 12.
- Cash needed at move-in = first rent payment + deposit + agency fee + connections and related charges + furniture, moving, and related costs.
- Actual monthly cash flow follows cheque and school fee payment dates rather than monthly-equivalent expenses.

Treat the security deposit as money held on deposit, and record the refund conditions, deductions, and settlement timing in the contract. When budgeting for a move, prepare the new home's deposit first and record the old home's refund on the date it is expected to be received.

## AD-014 Viewings, Tenancy Contracts, and Handover

### Facilities to Inspect During a Viewing

- Cooling performance, dampness, smells, and noise.
- Water pressure, drainage, and hot water.
- Furniture and appliance inventory, working condition, and responsibility for repairs.
- Number and location of parking spaces, and visitor parking.
- Gym and pool access terms.
- Available internet connections.
- Service lift, permitted moving hours, and move-in permission.

### Matters Set Out in the Contract

Annual rent, contract term, number and dates of payments, deposit refund conditions, agency fee, repair responsibilities, renewal, early termination, and additional charges for cooling, parking, and other items.

For a furnished property, obtain an inventory of furniture, appliances, tableware, and bedding from the management company, then buy missing items. Use the inventory again for the move-out settlement.

### Buying Furniture

Buy furniture at IKEA Abu Dhabi on Yas Island.

### Records at Move-In

Record the handover of keys, access cards, and parking, along with fixtures, faults, scratches, and meter readings, using photographs and written records. Recording existing damage reduces disagreements about deposit deductions at move-out.

Before paying, match the bank account name against the contracting party or the documents authorising an agent to receive payment, and obtain a receipt.

## AD-015 Tawtheeq and AccessRP

### Tawtheeq

Residential tenancy registration in Abu Dhabi's general jurisdiction. After signing the contract, ask the landlord or management company to register it and obtain the issued registration document.

### ADGM AccessRP

Properties on Al Reem Island and Al Maryah Island are subject to procedures through ADGM AccessRP.

- The landlord, authorised representative, or property management company starts tenancy registration.
- The tenant reviews and approves the details.
- ADGM guidance assigns the tenancy registration fee to the landlord.
- The platform integrates with electricity and water services and ICP.

## AD-016 Electricity, Water, and Municipality Charges

**TAQA Distribution** provides electricity and water in Abu Dhabi. The former ADDC and AADC have operated under the unified brand since 2025, so older documents still contain the previous names.

After Tawtheeq registration, check the TAQA supply account information and connection instructions. If these have not arrived, prepare the tenancy registration document and apply to TAQA for connection. Confirm the service start date before moving in.

### Municipality Fee

The fee is calculated at 5% of the higher of the contractual rent or rental index value and divided across monthly bills. The rental index is a government-system calculation representing an estimated rental value for the property.

- Annual reference amount × 5% = annual municipality fee.
- Annual municipality fee / 12 = monthly charge.
- TAQA bills can include items beyond electricity and water usage charges.
- For ADGM properties, obtain the property's annual and monthly billing items from the management company and use those amounts in the budget.

## AD-017 Cooling and Home Internet

### Cooling

Depending on the building, cooling costs are paid through the electricity bill or through a separate district cooling contract and bill.

The main cooling cost components are fixed charges, usage charges, connection fees, and a deposit. Confirm at contract signing whether the landlord or tenant pays them.

### Internet

Compare providers and connections available in the building, contract terms, installation dates, and early termination fees. Mobile data can cover the period before installation. Arrange an individual contract unless the landlord or residential management company has a building-wide contract. du and Etisalat are major providers.

## AD-018 Personal Bank Accounts

Resident current accounts require identity verification, residency, and income or source-of-funds information. ADCB, for example, lists a passport, residence permit, Emirates ID, and salary certificate among its required documents. Wio Personal is a convenient digital bank where an account can often be opened using only Emirates ID.

### Relationship to Rental Payments

There is a gap between submitting the account application, approval, and receiving the cheque book. If cheques will not be ready by the tenancy signing date, agree on an alternative payment method with the landlord in advance.

For international transfers, check the exchange rate, transfer fees, and expected arrival date, and schedule the transfer to arrive in time for the rent payment. To receive business revenue, obtain the bank's business account document requirements and proceed with opening the account.

## AD-020 Using Healthcare Services and Continuing Treatment

When settling in, identify a nearby clinic covered by the insurance plan, a paediatrician for the family, and a facility available at night.

- Use the insurance card or membership information in the app.
- Search the insurance app for facilities included in the plan and book with one of them.
- Certain tests and treatments require pre-authorisation.
- For ongoing treatment, bring medical records, test results, and medication active ingredients and dosages to make the transition easier.
- Active ingredient names are useful because medicine brand names can differ between the home country and the UAE.

## AD-021 Schools, Curricula, and Admissions

ADEK handles information on Abu Dhabi private schools. School selection involves curriculum, fees, ratings, available places, support provision, and travel to school.

### School Examples

- **Repton Abu Dhabi:** A British-curriculum school on Al Reem Island. Rose Campus is listed for Nursery to Year 1, and Fry Campus for Years 2 to 13. Compare travel distances using the campus the child will attend.
- **Cranleigh Abu Dhabi:** A British-curriculum school on Saadiyat Island. An option for families considering living on Saadiyat.

### Admission Process

Enquire about availability → year or grade placement and required assessments → application → admission offer → required payments → document submission → enrolment.

Obtain an admission offer and the payment terms needed to secure the place, then complete the admission process. Choose housing with the route to the child's campus in mind.

## AD-022 School Documents, Education Costs, and Transport

### Main Documents

Passport, residency and Emirates ID, birth certificate, school reports, enrolment or transfer certificates, and vaccination records. Required documents depend on age, the previous school's country, and the timing of the transfer, so contact the school early.

### Expenses Beyond Tuition

- Application, assessment, and admission fees.
- School bus.
- Uniforms, learning materials, and devices.
- Meals and extracurricular activities.

An annual school fee divided by 12 gives a monthly budget figure, while actual payments follow the school's termly schedule. Employer education benefits vary in the number of children covered, eligible expenses, limits, and reimbursement timing.

Check school bus coverage areas, stops, and journey times when choosing housing. For school runs by car, congestion around the school and the subsequent route to work affect daily travel time.

## AD-023 Driving Licences

Abu Dhabi Mobility / TAMM is the gateway for driving licences in Abu Dhabi. Foreign licence exchange depends on the issuing country, personal eligibility such as nationality, and licence validity. Licences from countries such as Japan can be exchanged. Applicants outside the exchange scheme follow the driving lessons and tests route.

### Documents and Procedures for Licence Exchange

- The applicant's valid foreign driving licence.
- Passport and Emirates ID.
- The required eye test.
- The applicable official translation, obtained from the relevant country's embassy or consulate in the UAE.

The exchange fee is AED600.

## AD-025 Darb Tolls

Abu Dhabi's Darb toll is **AED4 per chargeable crossing**.

| Gate | Charging Hours |
|---|---|
| Sas Al Nakhl, Al Maqta, Rabdan, Al Saadiyat | 7-9 am and 3-7 pm |
| Al Qurm, Ghantout | 24-hour charging window |

Sundays and official public holidays are excluded from charging. Match the gate on the route to the table above and calculate the charge based on the planned crossing time.

Toll cost = number of chargeable crossings × AED4. Commuting costs depend on the gates crossed and the time of travel.

Dubai has a similar system called Salik. Darb is a separate system, and journeys between the two cities can incur charges under both.

## AD-026 Mawaqif Parking

| Category | Kerb Colours | Fee | Maximum Stay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premium | White and turquoise | AED3/hour | 4 hours |
| Standard | Black and turquoise | AED2/hour or AED15/day | 24 hours |

- Standard paid hours are 8 am to midnight.
- Sundays and official public holidays are listed as free.
- Resident-only spaces require a resident permit from 9 pm to 8 am the following day.
- Without a resident permit, use general spaces or available facility parking during resident-only hours.
- Building parking, commercial facility parking, and public Mawaqif parking have separate usage conditions.
